# Changelog

## chainmax-2k 1.0.0 — expansion and GitHub publication preparation

Expanded the target to 2,000 unique base problems, 200 per family and 50 per
family/level cell. Preserved the original 1,000 business and solver artifacts with
explicit dataset-migration provenance. Added configurable audit quotas and 200
stratified second reviews. At the owner's request, the repository now includes
the complete corpus, reference artifacts, audit evidence and archive. Human review
remains pending; publication receipts record actual remote actions separately.

## 1.0.0 — local preparation, unpublished

Initial chainmax-2k construction tooling, ten synthetic families, bounded integer
IR, binary/PB/WCNF compiler, independent CP-SAT backend and business checker,
exhaustive and mutation tests, audit and packaging workflow. Human description
review remains required for final release. No publication date is asserted.
